# Roadmap

The playable slice covers five handcrafted waves. Headless tests cover two winning opening strategies, an undefended loss, deterministic outcomes, and wave-report accounting.

1. Playtest the five-wave slice with new players. Record the opening formation, core damage by lane, scrap earned, and where players struggle to explain a loss. Compare defense-first and economy openings before tuning costs or rewards.
2. Improve combat readability and presentation. Test whether stronger silhouettes and optional attack sounds help players identify targets and breaches even at 2× speed.
3. Prototype one functional defender upgrade and one era transition. Check whether the retrofit creates a reason to retain an early unit and a meaningful alternative to buying another defender.
4. Try a second battlefield or wave set once players can win the first slice with multiple approaches.
5. Evaluate command serialization and replay determinism before considering multiplayer.
