# Roadmap

See the [development plan](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) for milestone scope and verification. These playtest priorities apply to the current campaign.

The current single-player run spans ten waves and two eras. Its main design bets are seven-pad formations, leashed interceptors, positional links, branching constructs, fusion, and telegraphed waves that reward formation changes.

1. Playtest Milestone 2A through a loss or victory. Observe whether players find the construct branches, discover adjacency, and understand the fusion inputs and costs. Compare keeping two specialists with consolidating their roles.
2. Tune late-game spending and the Bastion–Vanguard tradeoff against the headless sweep and human play. Track unspent scrap and the effect of a faster clear versus a safer formation.
3. Review Milestone 2A feedback before starting the roster and combination expansion.
