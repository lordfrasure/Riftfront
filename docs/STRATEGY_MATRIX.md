## Dominance and redundancy

- **Verified under tested policies.** Early single salvage is low risk and profitable. Skipping it can still win cleanly. Two salvage is risky in the tested package, which also changes the frontline. Bastion is favored across the legacy sweep, but Vanguard is viable with healing. No upgrade has demonstrated universal superiority. The legacy planner also forces selected center-wave and rear-slot upgrades, so branch labels describe a preference.
- **Verified dispensability.** A perfect economy clear uses neither Saboteur nor Coilcaster. A fusion-oriented army can omit fusion and still defend the core. These units and mechanics therefore lack exclusivity as answers in the fixed campaign. They have distinct effects, and the data does not establish any recruit as universally redundant.
- **Hypotheses to test.** Saboteur may be squeezed by general-purpose pierce and exposure. Anchor may compete unevenly with Bastion once Coil unlocks, while Mortar may compete with immediate Lobber purchases. Fusion's pad advantage may be too weak where the tested armies finish with 6–9 free pads. Test matched encounter and budget choices before removing or buffing any unit.
